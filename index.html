Top 5 "Test Traps" to Watch Out For!

Read these 5 secrets before the test so you don't lose easy marks!

Trap #1 • Exercise 6 Spelling

12th, 29th & February!

• 12th = twelfth (drop the ve, write f!)
• 29th = twenty-ninth (no e in ninth!)
• Month 2 = February (don't forget the first r: Feb-r-uary!)

Trap #2 • Exercise 5 (#5)

"Rafi is sometimes busy"

Adverbs of frequency go BEFORE normal verbs (I always walk), but they go AFTER am / is / are!
✅ Rafi is sometimes busy on Saturday mornings.

Trap #3 • Exercise 7 (#2 & #6)

Don't put "-s" after I or You!

In Exercise 7, most sentences use He/She (Tessa sets, Sam takes, Dad does, Freddie makes, Lucy feeds), EXCEPT:
• #2: I tidy my room.
• #6: You vacuum the floor.

Trap #4 • Exercise 4 (#1 & #2)

Does + Base Verb (No -s!)

When you make a question with does, the main verb goes back to normal without -s:
✅ What time does the train leave?
✅ Does Xu go to your school?

Trap #5 • Exercise 5 (#2) "not often"

How to use "not often" (*) in a sentence

For "We go to the park in winter. (*)", because not often is negative, in English we add don't before the verb:
✅ We don't often go to the park in winter. (Our checker also recognizes if your class writes We not often go..., and shows you both!)
